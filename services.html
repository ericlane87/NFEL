Services

Institutional advisory services for wealth, banking, trade finance, and strategic capital readiness.

N.F.EL Strategic Alliances helps owners, family enterprises, importers, exporters, and cross-border investors organize the full financial picture: personal wealth, operating liquidity, banking relationships, transaction documentation, and risk.

Capabilities

Service boards built for clear decisions.

01 Trade Finance Advisory Import/export structuring, letters of credit, guarantees, receivables, and transaction controls.

N.F.EL helps clients prepare and structure cross-border transactions with the documentation and bank-facing clarity required for serious trade finance conversations.

  • Commercial letters of credit, standby letters of credit, documentary collections, and guarantees.
  • Receivables finance, supply-chain finance, purchase-order finance, and export support.
  • Review of payment terms, counterparties, trade corridors, timing, and transaction-specific risk.
02 Wealth Advisory & Owner Strategy Preservation planning for business owners whose personal and operating finances are connected.

This service connects personal balance sheet planning with business liquidity, investment planning, and long-term family enterprise objectives.

  • Liquidity-event preparation, investment planning, portfolio coordination, and risk profiling.
  • Succession readiness and coordination with tax, legal, insurance, and banking advisors.
  • Ongoing financial stewardship for owners with domestic and cross-border exposure.
03 Trust & Fiduciary Coordination Estate, trust, beneficiary, and family-enterprise planning coordinated with outside professionals.

N.F.EL can help organize trust and fiduciary conversations so ownership, inheritance, liquidity, banking, and business continuity planning are handled with discipline.

  • Coordination with estate attorneys, trustees, tax advisors, insurers, and private banking relationships.
  • Review of trust objectives, beneficiary needs, asset location, succession timing, and liquidity requirements.
  • Documentation readiness for clients preparing generational wealth and business continuity plans.
04 Commercial Banking & Treasury Deposit strategy, working capital, treasury management, and relationship banking readiness.

The banking work is built around cash visibility, credit readiness, operating liquidity, and stronger presentation to commercial banking partners.

  • Working-capital lines, credit facilities, deposits, treasury management, and cash conversion analysis.
  • Review of DSO, DPO, inventory cycles, purchase commitments, and seasonal liquidity needs.
  • Preparation for lender conversations, banking relationship reviews, and financial documentation requests.
05 Credit Enhancement Structures Commercial proof-of-funds and balance-sheet enhancement support for funding conversations.

This service supports clients evaluating credit enhancement structures involving seasoned instruments and commercial funding readiness.

  • Proof-of-funds and balance-sheet enhancement positioning for qualified commercial use cases.
  • Readiness review for bank guarantees, standby letters of credit, bonds, MTNs, and commercial paper.
  • Guidance on borrower readiness, instrument verification, lender procedure, maturity, and collateral-use documentation.
06 Risk, Compliance & Documentation KYC, AML, sanctions awareness, country-risk review, and transaction integrity controls.

Documentation discipline protects client credibility. N.F.EL organizes the intake, risk review, and controls needed before sensitive financial or trade materials are advanced.

  • KYC readiness, AML considerations, sanctions awareness, and country-risk review.
  • Trade documentation controls, counterparty review, data protection, and escalation of material risks.
  • Client portal workflow for onboarding, document submission, AI-assisted deal evaluation, and reporting.
07 Strategic Alliances & Partner Channels Relationship coordination across banks, attorneys, insurers, logistics partners, and referral channels.

N.F.EL helps coordinate the professionals and institutions around the client so the advisory, banking, tax, legal, insurance, logistics, and trade finance work moves in the same direction.

  • Partner coordination with banks, private bankers, attorneys, tax advisors, insurers, customs brokers, and freight forwarders.
  • Referral relationship development, partner memoranda, transaction case positioning, and market-entry support.
  • Execution support for commercial projects, international corridors, and strategic growth opportunities.
